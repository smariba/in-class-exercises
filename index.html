Dusty Toes Alpaca & Llama Welfare, Education & Research

Caring for camelids — and sharing what they teach us.

We are a group of volunteers supporting farmers with care and programming for llamas and alpacas
in situations when the farmer is not able to — and we are building a nonprofit to give them a sustainable home,
and to develop programming that benefits the animals and the people who spend time with them.

Get to Know Us

Who we are, how this started, and the sixty animals at the centre of it. Dusty is eighteen, the matriarch of our
trekking team, and the reason any of this exists.

Visit Us

Treks, tours, events and programs, all at the pace the animals set. Everything is arranged in advance, because
this is a working farm and somebody’s home.

Support Us

Hay, pellets, supplements and veterinary care for an ageing herd. Give funds or goods, adopt a camelid, or
sponsor a program for the season.

Get Involved

Muck a barn, mend a fence, help on a trek, or lend a skill from home. We welcome volunteers of all abilities
and backgrounds — caring for a herd takes every kind of hand.

Learn

What camelids are, what they are telling you, and material for teachers. Sharing what we learn is half of what
we do.

Shop

Fibre from this herd and the things our volunteers make from it. Everything sold goes straight back into the
animals’ care.
